IU ACM Student Chapter

Welcome to the official website of the Islamic University of Madinah ACM Student Chapter: A community of passionate Computer Science and Engineering students, driven by curiosity, code, and cutting-edge technology. We welcome students of all backgrounds, interests, and skill levels to join our community and share our love for technology.

Our Groups

Teams focus on specific, highly technical areas of computer science and engineering.

<CP_Team />

Competitive Programming

Mastering algorithms, data structures, and rigorous problem-solving to excel in collegiate contests and competitive coding platforms.

<AI_Team />

Artificial Intelligence

Exploring machine learning theory, modern deep learning architectures, and collaborative data science projects.

<Robotics_Team />

Robotics

Bridging software logic with physical systems through microcontrollers, sensor arrays, and embedded hardware development.

Executive_Board

Executive Board

The core leadership team responsible for strategic decision-making, official university correspondence, and overall chapter management.

Advisory_Mentorship

Advisory & Mentorship

A board of industry professionals, alumni, and senior students bridging the gap between academia and industry demands.

IT_Infrastructure

IT & Infrastructure

Responsible for developing, operating, and maintaining the chapter's technical infrastructure, including internal systems and the official website.

Media_Design

Media & Design

Tasked with developing the chapter's visual identity, managing social media, and documenting events, workshops, and activities.
